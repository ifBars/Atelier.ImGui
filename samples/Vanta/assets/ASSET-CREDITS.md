# Vanta assets

- `nebula.jpg`: Bubble Nebula image, credit **NASA, ESA, and the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA)**. [Original NASA image and credits](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/hubble/bubble-nebula-ngc-7635/). Downloaded from [the Unsplash image CDN](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1462331940025-496dfbfc7564?w=1400&q=90&fit=crop). Cropping, tint and overlay graphics happen at render time.
- `Rajdhani-SemiBold.ttf`: Rajdhani from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/rajdhani), under the SIL Open Font License. The downloaded license is included as `OFL.txt`.

The image is a real astronomical photograph. The ship, orbit indicators and other UI graphics are original sample draw commands; this example is not affiliated with NASA, ESA or the referenced game-menu products.
